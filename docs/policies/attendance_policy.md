# Attendance Policy

All employees are expected to maintain at least 90% monthly attendance,
excluding approved leave. Absences are tracked in three categories:

- **Sick days** — self-reported, no manager pre-approval required, but HR is
  notified automatically after 3 consecutive sick days.
- **Unplanned absences** — any absence without 24 hours' notice that isn't
  sick leave. These are the strongest signal in our attendance-risk model;
  employees with high unplanned-absence rates are flagged for a manager
  check-in, not disciplinary action.
- **Approved leave** — vacation, parental leave, bereavement, etc., booked
  in advance. Does not count against attendance rate.

An employee is marked **high absenteeism risk** when the attendance model
predicts elevated risk for the current period, based on attendance rate,
unplanned-absence frequency, overtime load, and tenure. This is a signal
for proactive support (workload check, flexibility options), not an
automatic escalation — managers are expected to have a conversation before
any formal process starts.

New hires (under 6 months' tenure) are given a wider tolerance band, since
onboarding naturally produces more irregular schedules.
