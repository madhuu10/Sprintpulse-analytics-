# Remote Work (WFH) Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week without prior approval.
WFH days are logged the same as office days for attendance-rate purposes —
they count as present, not absent.

Teams with sprint commitments are expected to keep standup attendance
(video or in-person) above 90% regardless of WFH status. Standup
attendance is tracked separately from overall attendance and is one of the
inputs to the sprint-performance model, since ceremony attendance tends to
track with how synchronized a team stays during a sprint.

Extended remote arrangements (more than 2 days/week) go through the
employee's manager and are not reflected in this dataset.
