# Sprint Process

Sprints run two weeks, Monday to the second following Friday. Planning
happens the first morning; retro happens the last afternoon.

**Scope changes mid-sprint** are tracked as `scope_change_pct` — the share
of a sprint's final committed points that were added after planning
closed. This is one of the strongest negative predictors in the
sprint-performance model: teams that hold scope changes under ~10% finish
close to their original commitment far more often than teams that don't.
Any addition after day 2 should go through the product manager and be
flagged in standup, not quietly added to the board.

**Carryover** (`carryover_points_in`) — points not completed last sprint
get re-estimated and added to the next sprint's commitment. High
persistent carryover across several sprints is a planning-accuracy
problem, not an execution problem, and should be raised with the PM
rather than treated as a team performance issue.

The target completion rate (points completed / points committed) across
teams is roughly 85-95%. Below 60% is treated as "off track" and warrants
a retro deep-dive; above ~110% usually means the team under-committed
relative to capacity, not that they over-performed.
